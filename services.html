Our Impact

Beyond great coffee and cashews — we're building a better future for Tanzanian farmers and communities.

Improving Income for Smallholder Farmers

By sourcing directly from farmers, we ensure they receive fair and stable prices, helping them increase their earnings and improve their livelihoods.

Reducing Post-Harvest Losses

We collect products quickly and store them properly, preventing spoilage and waste that often affect farmers during peak harvest seasons.

Strengthening Rural Economies

Our work creates reliable demand, supports local jobs, and keeps more value within farming communities across Tanzania.

Promoting Sustainable Practices

We encourage clean processing, responsible harvesting, and better handling methods that protect both the environment and product quality.

Expanding Global Market Access

By delivering consistent quality, we help position Tanzanian coffee and cashew as competitive products in regional and global markets.
