About Us

We are a dedicated supplier of premium coffee and high-quality cashew sourced directly from trusted farmers across Tanzania. Our mission is to deliver fresh, well-graded products with authentic flavor and consistent quality—backed by reliable service for every customer.

Our Story

Proplus was founded to bridge the gap between farmers and buyers. We travel to the fields, work closely with growers, and ensure every batch meets market standards. This hands-on approach helps us support rural communities while supplying clean, traceable products to businesses.

Our Commitment

We are committed to fair sourcing, honest pricing, and strong partnerships. Our focus is on quality at every step—from collection and grading to storage and delivery—so customers receive products they can trust, order after order.

Vision

To become Tanzania’s most trusted source of premium coffee and cashew, connecting farmers to stable markets and delivering quality products worldwide.

Mission

To collect, grade, and supply fresh, high-quality agricultural products by working directly with farmers, ensuring fair trade and reliable service.

Our Values

  • Quality – Strict standards from farm to delivery
  • Integrity – Honesty, fairness, and transparency
  • Partnership – Long-term relationships with farmers & buyers
  • Reliability – Consistent products and dependable service

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Where do you source your coffee and cashew?

We collect our products directly from trusted farmers and cooperatives to ensure consistent quality and fair prices.

2. Do you supply your products in bulk?

Yes. We supply in bulk to wholesalers, exporters, cafés, processors, and large retailers with flexible order quantities.

3. Can I request specific grades or quality levels?

Absolutely — you can request custom grades, moisture levels, and packaging to match your business needs.

4. How do you ensure the quality of your product

Every batch is carefully graded, moisture-tested, and stored in clean conditions to meet strict quality standards.
