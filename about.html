Built for safe, reliable access to health essentials

Pro Plus exists to make pharmaceutical and health products easier to access. We focus on quality, safe handling, and customer support so people can buy with confidence and get what they need without stress.

Our Story

Pro Plus started with a simple problem: people spend too much time searching for trusted products, comparing prices, and worrying about authenticity. We created a reliable process that prioritizes verified sourcing, good storage, and support so customers can order quickly and safely.

Vision

To become Tanzania’s most trusted health and pharmacy platform, improving access to safe, affordable, and genuine health products.

Mission

To provide quality-assured pharmaceutical and health products, supported by clear guidance and reliable service, so individuals and families can access health essentials easily and safely.

Our Values

  • Quality – Verified products and safe handling
  • Integrity – Honest pricing and transparency for eveyone
  • Care Support and responsible guidance
  • ReliabilityConsistent service and fulfillment
